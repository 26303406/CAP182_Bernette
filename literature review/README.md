# Research Studies

This folder contains the academic and industry research used to support the CAP182 project. It provides the literature foundation for the study of customer activation, fintech engagement, and predictive modelling in a digital investment context.

## Purpose

The research in this folder supports the project by examining how digital financial services convert new users into active customers, why onboarding and engagement drop-off occurs, and how behavioural, demographic, and communication data can be used to improve activation outcomes.

## Included resources

- 26303406_Bernette_CAP182_SS2_Part A Literature Review.pdf
  The project literature review covering fintech adoption, onboarding friction, customer retention, and predictive analytics.

## Main themes

- customer acquisition versus customer activation
- fintech onboarding behaviour and abandonment risk
- predictive modelling in financial customer analytics
- engagement, retention, and churn reduction in digital platforms
- segmentation and intervention strategies for growth and conversion improvement

## Relevance to the project

This folder grounds the project in existing research and supports the business case for using data-driven methods to identify customers likely to remain inactive, improve onboarding journeys, and increase activation through more targeted interventions.
